# Footballer Quest — Trait Interaction Rules

**Status:** APPROVED_CONCEPT / Director-frozen structural guardrails; exact balance values remain simulation work  
**Milestone:** M2 Traits/Synergies foundation  
**Purpose:** define how Element, Team and Faction Traits interact when several effects touch the same combat axis, and prevent loops that only appear in mixed builds.

This document supplements `TRAITS_SYNERGIES_V1.md`, `ELEMENT_TRAITS_V1.md`, `TRAIT_PILOTS.md`, `AFFILIATION_TRAIT_MODEL.md` and `TRAIT_DNA_RULES.md`.

The goal is **not** to limit the number of active Traits artificially. A player who pays the roster, recruitment and DNA opportunity cost may legitimately activate several Traits. The rules below instead prevent multiple Traits from multiplying the same underlying axis into an automatic dominant build.

---

## 1. Tempo / priority resolution

Aria, Gemini Storm and Diamond Dust may all touch timing, priority or control, but they must keep distinct identities:

- **Aria** -> recover/manipulate tempo and create short timing windows;
- **Gemini Storm** -> voluntary switch + initiative + Momentum;
- **Diamond Dust** -> Freeze/control and the preserve-vs-Shatter decision.

When several active effects modify the **same timing axis** in one action window:

1. resolve the strongest applicable priority/timing advantage as the primary modifier;
2. at most one additional authored control modifier may also apply when explicitly compatible;
3. do not silently add multiple priority effects until first action becomes effectively guaranteed;
4. any exception that intentionally stacks must be authored, visible and simulation-tested.

This is an interaction rule, not a global ban on multiple active Traits.

### Corrente Favorevole trigger definition

`Aria Tier I — Corrente Favorevole` should represent genuinely losing tempo to the opponent.

It must **not** trigger merely because the player deliberately:

- used a voluntary switch;
- spent an action entering `Punto Fermo`;
- lost an action through another deliberate sacrifice/cost;
- replaced a player after KO.

Working direction: Corrente Favorevole becomes eligible when the opponent legitimately wins a normal action-order exchange through SPD, priority or authored control.

---

## 2. Hard-control resolution

Confusion, Freeze and future control states must not create stacked independent "you do not act" rolls.

For one attempted action:

- resolve at most **one true action-denial check**;
- additional compatible control effects may still modify SPD, priority, effectiveness or other readable timing outputs;
- do not chain multiple independent skip/fail rolls from several statuses/Traits on the same attempted action.

This preserves control builds without creating permanent or near-permanent hard locks.

---

## 3. Terra anti-loop rules

### Stratificazione / Brace

A hit already mitigated by a `Brace` generated by `Terra Tier II — Stratificazione` must **not** immediately generate a new Brace from that same hit.

Likewise, a hit strongly mitigated by `Terra Tier III — Punto Fermo` must not generate a new Stratificazione Brace from the mitigated event.

Purpose: prevent `Brace -> hit -> new Brace -> hit -> new Brace` defensive loops.

### Contrappeso after Punto Fermo

`Terra Tier I — Contrappeso` may still become eligible after a valid enemy impact absorbed through Punto Fermo, provided its ordinary trigger conditions are satisfied.

This interaction is intentional:

`predict impact -> anchor -> absorb -> answer with counterpressure`.

Contrappeso remains non-stackable and is still lost on voluntary switch under the Element Trait rules.

---

## 4. Fuoco anti-abuse rules

### Combustione and multi-hit actions

`Fuoco Tier II — Combustione` may advance **at most one future Burn tick per eligible technique/action**.

A multi-hit technique must not advance one Burn tick per individual hit.

The advanced tick is still removed from Burn's future scheduled damage; Combustione accelerates damage, it does not duplicate it.

### Self-inflicted costs do not count as enemy pressure by default

HP recoil from `Fuoco Tier III — Surriscaldamento`, Alius Overdrive or another authored self-cost is a **cost**, not an opponent-caused hit.

It must not automatically trigger effects whose fantasy is reacting to enemy pressure, including comeback/pressure triggers such as `Spirito Raimon`, unless a future rule explicitly says otherwise.

This prevents deliberate self-damage from becoming a free activation engine for unrelated Traits.

---

## 5. Natura — Germoglio and Fioritura interaction rules

### Germoglio ownership

`Germoglio` is a **battle-local team resource** for an active Natura build, not a permanent run resource and not a multi-stack Momentum bar.

Approved structural direction:

- maximum one Germoglio charge at a time;
- an eligible Natura recovery action can create it;
- another eligible Natura player may later consume it through Fioritura while the required Trait remains active;
- Germoglio does not accumulate multiple charges;
- it is cleared at battle end;
- exact switching/KO edge cases remain implementation detail, but no duplication may occur.

### Fioritura does not recycle itself

Healing produced by `Natura Tier III — Fioritura`:

- does **not** generate a new Germoglio;
- is not re-multiplied by `Linfa Vitale` into a recursive healing loop;
- does not become shield, stored overheal, currency or revival value.

### Fioritura starting balance model

The following values are **approved simulation starting points**, not final Production numbers:

- initial healing pool = approximately **40% of actual direct damage dealt** by the empowered Natura technique;
- use actual HP removed from the target, so overkill damage does not create extra healing;
- cap the initial pool at approximately **40% of the acting player's Max HP**;
- heal the active player first;
- after the active player is healed, only **50% of the remaining pool** may spill to injured teammates; the other 50% of that remainder is lost;
- teammate distribution prioritises the lowest **remaining HP percentage**, not the largest absolute HP deficit;
- continue through injured teammates while transferable healing remains;
- any healing left when no eligible injured teammate remains is lost.

Example using the working numbers:

- Fioritura deals 200 actual damage -> initial pool 80;
- active player is missing 50 HP -> heals 50;
- 30 remains;
- only 50% of that remainder, 15, becomes teammate healing;
- the remaining 15 is lost even if other teammates are injured.

Purpose: Fioritura should be a strong emergency sustain play, not a full-roster reset comparable to a free between-battle heal.

No Fioritura heal may revive a KO player.

## 6. Same-axis damage/healing stacking

Mixed Element/Team/Faction builds may legitimately create several simultaneous modifiers.

Do not solve this by limiting the player to an arbitrary maximum number of active Traits.

Instead, effects that modify the **same numerical axis** should enter one controlled resolution layer rather than becoming an uncontrolled multiplicative chain.

Examples of same-axis groups include:

- direct damage/POT modifiers;
- healing multipliers;
- priority/timing modifiers;
- Guard/mitigation modifiers;
- status-application modifiers.

Preferred implementation direction:

- compose compatible same-axis bonuses in an additive or otherwise bounded authored model;
- apply caps/ordering where simulation proves necessary;
- reserve multiplicative stacking for explicitly authored interactions with a clear reason;
- surface special stacking behaviour in UI/tooltips when it materially changes a decision.

A future build such as Fuoco Pressure + Terra Contrappeso + Raimon comeback may therefore receive all legitimately active effects, but the engine must not blindly resolve them as an exponential `x1.15 x1.20 x1.30 ...` chain.

---

## 7. Team + Faction overlap

The existing affiliation rule remains in force:

- Team Traits own sharper tactical specialization;
- Faction Traits own broader cross-team/run/risk-reward identity;
- the same trigger must not silently receive two large copies of the same reward merely because a CharacterVersion satisfies both Team and Faction identity.

Alius remains the first approved proof case.

---

## 8. Trait reachability is not guaranteed progression

Trait tiers are composition thresholds, not automatic level-up rewards.

A run may legitimately end with:

- only Tier I of an intended build;
- several low/mid Traits instead of one Tier III;
- a near-threshold state such as `3 / 4` that is never completed;
- no tier of a desired Trait if recruitment/DNA decisions never create sufficient contribution.

This is desirable **only if the run actually offered fair build opportunities**. A Trait advertised as buildable must not be practically unreachable because of hidden rarity/scenario/role constraints.

The current `2 / 4 / 6` thresholds remain a simulation candidate, not frozen Production balance.

---

## 9. Coverage Audit extension

Trait coverage/reachability simulation should include **Element Traits as well as Team/Faction Traits**.

For each relevant Trait, future audits should report where possible:

- probability of reaching Tier I / II / III;
- starter contribution;
- role distribution;
- rarity/accessibility;
- scenario/arc availability;
- earliest and median acquisition timing;
- DNA contribution and hybrid paths;
- common mixed-Trait overlaps;
- compositions that become near-mandatory or dominant;
- compositions that appear supported in UI but are practically unreachable.

DNA simulations must retain the approved `1.0 primary / 0.5 secondary` contribution and same-element deduplication rules.

---

## 10. Approved interaction summary

The v1 interaction philosophy is:

- **many Traits may coexist** when the build earns them;
- **same-axis effects do not multiply without control**;
- **tempo/control cannot chain into guaranteed action denial**;
- **Terra cannot recursively regenerate Brace from its own mitigation**;
- **Fuoco cannot farm Burn ticks through multi-hit or use self-recoil as generic enemy pressure**;
- **Natura cannot turn Fioritura into a recursive or full-roster reset engine**;
- **Team/Faction overlap remains explicit and non-duplicative**;
- **Tier reachability is a run outcome, not a promise — but practical build access must be audited**.
